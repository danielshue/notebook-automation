# Productivity Workflows

While Notebook Automation is powerful for academic use, it is equally effective for professional and personal productivity. This guide covers workflows for meetings, technical research, and content creation.

## 1. Meeting Minute Automation

Transform recordings of Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet calls into structured meeting minutes.

### The Problem
Manually taking notes during meetings distracts from participation, and key details are often lost.

### The Workflow

1. **Record the Meeting**: Save the video/audio file from your meeting platform.
2. **Process with CLI**:
   ```bash
   na video-notes -p "meetings/2023-10-27-Project-Sync.mp4" --no-quiz
   ```
   *Note: We use `--no-quiz` because we don't need study questions for a business meeting.*

3. **Result**: A Markdown file containing:
   - **Executive Summary**: Generated by AI.
   - **Full Transcript**: Searchable text of who said what.
   - **Action Items**: (If configured in prompt) extracted tasks.

### Pro Tip: Custom Template
Create a `meeting-note` template that emphasizes **Action Items** and **Decisions Made** over "Key Concepts".

## 2. Technical Research & Whitepapers

Ingest complex technical documentation, whitepapers, or spec sheets to build a searchable knowledge base.

### The Problem
Technical details are scattered across dozens of PDFs, making it hard to find specific specifications or architecture decisions.

### The Workflow

1. **Bulk Ingest**:
   ```bash
   na pdf-notes -p "research/architecture-docs/" --extract-images
   ```
2. **Consolidate Tags**:
   Organize by technology or domain.
   ```bash
   na tag add-nested "research/architecture-docs/"
   ```
3. **Generate Index**:
   Create a Map of Content (MOC) to browse the documents.
   ```bash
   na vault generate-index "research/architecture-docs/"
   ```

### Benefit
You can now ask your vault (via Obsidian Copilot or search) questions like *"What is the retry policy defined in the v2 specs?"* and get answers linked to the source PDF.

## 3. Content Repurposing (YouTube to Blog)

Creators can use the tool to turn video content into written articles or newsletters.

### The Workflow

1. **Download Video**: Get your raw video file.
2. **Generate Full Transcript**:
   ```bash
   na video-notes -p "videos/my-video.mp4"
   ```
3. **Drafting**:
   - Open the generated Markdown file.
   - Use the **AI Summary** as your intro.
   - Use the **Key Topics** as your section headers.
   - Copy/paste polished segments from the **Transcript** to flesh out the body.

This turns a 20-minute video into a blog post draft in minutes rather than hours.
